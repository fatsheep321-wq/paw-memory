# PawStory species, awakening and printable QR

- Three original procedural sample species: dog, cat and alpaca; no claim of photo reconstruction or printable pet meshes.
- Awakening stages: monochrome model, color/light sweep, supplied photograph, video. Video starts only in the final stage, with native controls on autoplay failure. Reduced-motion preference skips transitions.
- The same target URL drives the object preview, PNG and relief STL. Unbound accessories use the clearly labelled fixed demo; bound accessories use their published memory URL and accessory query.
- The downloadable STL is a separate 60 × 60 × 2.8 mm QR plaque: 2 mm backing, 0.8 mm raised dark modules, four-module quiet zone. It is not a pet STL or machine-specific G-code. Use a light backing and dark raised layer in a slicer; material contrast and scanning require physical validation.
- Module islands use a 0.002 mm inset to avoid diagonal non-manifold contacts. All connected relief surfaces share the backing. This is a geometric construction tolerance, not a claim of printer accuracy.
- Verification reimports exported STL, checks dimensions and two-face edge incidence, and decodes a top projection of its actual raised triangles. Fixed demo and a UUID birthday URL both pass.
