# Fabrication model modifications

PawStory's tag is based on structural ideas from scadqr `demo_tag.scad` at commit `a27e1feeed8b048b730fcd2620c0021b3b52a283` (Darwin Schuppan and contributors, MIT).

The project reimplements the model in TypeScript/Three.js as a 60 × 42 × 3 mm adjustable rounded plate with two through holes, a matching laser panel, a real memory URL QR code with four-module quiet zone, and outline-only ASCII engraving labels. Upstream SCAD is not bundled. The flexible-link reference grid is project-created and is not distributed as a manufacturing file.

Printer/material tolerance, laser settings, garment fit, strength, waterproofing, and pet-wear safety remain unverified and require physical prototyping. No G-code, laser power, or speed settings are supplied.
